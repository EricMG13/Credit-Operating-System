# CP-3D E — Credit handoff

State which risks appear priced, the most material curve or peer residual, implied break-even assumptions, observable liquidity constraint, freshness limit and the evidence that would close the fundamental-versus-market gap.

CP-3 consumes the map and retains security-selection ownership. CP-3A consumes structural/recovery pricing differences. CP-3B consumes executable-liquidity evidence. CP-6 receives the strongest market challenge to the fundamental thesis. Timestamped spread/price/liquidity thresholds remain available for a later independent CP-EMAIL run, but this artifact neither invokes CP-EMAIL nor creates a dependency edge.

CP-3D never emits buy/sell/hold, rich/cheap as a terminal recommendation, instrument rank or position size.
