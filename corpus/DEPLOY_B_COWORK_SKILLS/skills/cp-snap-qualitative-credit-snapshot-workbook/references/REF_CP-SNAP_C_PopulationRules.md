# REF CP-SNAP C — Population Rules

Populate only source-supported text owned by the mapped module.

- Company/sector/shareholders/country: short factual values from CP-1A.
- Transaction summary: concise transaction facts; omit vendor pricing fields.
- Business description: creditor-relevant operations, mix, geography and
  revenue drivers without promotional language.
- Historical performance: CP-1B period-specific performance and cash-flow
  trajectory, preserving cited qualifiers and comparability flags.
- Strengths/weaknesses: CP-2 credit synthesis, balanced and mechanism-led.
- Catalysts/events: CP-2B dated near-term events and monitoring triggers.

Respect the source's qualification and uncertainty. Do not add unsupported
ratings, prices, forecasts, recommendations or numerical model outputs.

Keep prose compact enough to fit the mapped region with the template's font and
wrap settings. If supported content is too long, prioritize the most material
credit points and record the omission in limitations; do not shrink or
reformat the template.
